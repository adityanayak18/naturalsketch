Privacy Policy

NaturalSketch turns sketches and descriptions into working web app prototypes. This page explains what we store about you, why, and what you can do about it, in plain words.

What we collect

  • Your account. Your email address. If you sign in with Google, also the name and profile photo Google shares. If you sign up with email, your password is stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider (Supabase); nobody, including us, can read it.
  • What you create. The descriptions, feature lists and images you upload, the chat messages you send, and the apps, versions and build logs that are generated for you.
  • Usage counts. How many builds and edits you make each day, so the daily allowance can be applied fairly.

We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics cookies.

How it's used

  • To sign you in and keep your apps private to your account.
  • To build and edit your apps. To do this, your descriptions, images and messages are sent to the AI model providers that generate the code: Google Gemini, Groq and OpenRouter. Their own privacy policies apply to that processing. Please don't upload anything confidential or anyone's personal information.

Where it's stored

Account and project data are stored with Supabase (database and sign-in). The site is served by Vercel and the application server runs on Render. These providers process data on our behalf to run the service.

Cookies and browser storage

We use one essential cookie to keep you signed in, and your browser stores your sign-in session. Apps you generate keep their demo data (sample products, carts, logins and so on) in your own browser's local storage, not on our servers.

Who can see your apps

Only you can see your projects in the studio. A generated app's preview is reachable by anyone who has its exact preview link, so share those links only with people you want to see the app.

Your choices

  • You can delete any of your apps from the studio at any time; this deletes its versions, uploads and chats.
  • To delete your whole account, or to ask what we hold about you, email us at the address below and we'll take care of it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Questions: naturalsketchadmin@gmail.com.
